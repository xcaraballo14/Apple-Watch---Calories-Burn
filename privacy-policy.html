PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: June 15, 2025  ·  Last Updated: June 15, 2025

1. OVERVIEW

BurnReward ("we", "our", or "the app") is a watchOS fitness application developed by Xavier Caraballo. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains in plain terms what information BurnReward accesses, why we access it, where it stays, and — critically — what we never do with it.

The short version: BurnReward reads your health data exclusively on your Apple Watch. It is processed locally on your device, never transmitted to any server, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising. We do not collect, store, or see your personal information.

2. INFORMATION WE ACCESS

BurnReward requests access to the following data types through Apple's HealthKit framework:

Data Type HealthKit Identifier Access Mode Why We Need It
Active Energy Burned HKQuantityTypeIdentifierActiveEnergyBurned READ The core metric — we compare your live calorie burn against your quest goal to show EXP progress and trigger reward milestones.
Heart Rate HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRate READ Displayed live during your workout as a real-time indicator, and averaged at workout end to populate your summary screen.
Workout Sessions HKWorkoutType READ WRITE We create an HKWorkoutSession to activate the watch's workout sensors at full fidelity and write the completed workout to Health when your quest is finished.

WHAT WE DO NOT ACCESS

BurnReward does not request and cannot access: body weight, blood oxygen, ECG data, sleep data, menstrual health data, reproductive health data, location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal or sensitive data type not listed above.

3. HOW DATA IS STORED

BurnReward stores the following data exclusively on your Apple Watch using Apple's standard on-device storage:

  • Workout progress (current calorie count, phase, elapsed time, selected rewards) — stored in UserDefaults on-device so your quest resumes if the app is closed mid-workout. This data is cleared when you start a new quest or tap "NEW QUEST."
  • Workout summary (total duration, average heart rate, total calories) — stored on-device after a quest is completed and displayed on the earned screen. Cleared when you start a new quest.
  • Complication snapshot (quest active/inactive, emoji, calorie progress) — written to an App Group container shared between the app and the watch-face complication widget. This stays on-device only.

None of this data is ever uploaded to a server, transmitted over a network, or stored in the cloud by BurnReward. It lives entirely on your watch.

Completed workouts saved to Apple Health are stored and managed entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy and iCloud data practices. BurnReward has no access to workout records after they are written.

4. HOW DATA IS USED

Data accessed by BurnReward is used solely for the following in-app purposes:

  • Tracking live active calorie burn against your selected food reward goal (EXP progress bar).
  • Displaying your real-time heart rate during a workout session.
  • Triggering haptic milestone notifications at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your calorie goal.
  • Computing and displaying a workout summary (duration, average heart rate, calories) when your quest is complete.
  • Saving the completed workout to Apple Health so it counts toward your rings and workout history.
  • Updating the watch-face complication to show live quest progress.

We do not use your health data for advertising, analytics, machine learning, profiling, research, or any purpose beyond the in-app features described above.

5. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

BurnReward does not share your data with anyone.

  • We do not have servers. There is no backend. No data leaves your device through BurnReward.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Firebase, Amplitude, Crashlytics, or equivalent).
  • We do not use any advertising SDKs or frameworks.
  • We do not sell, rent, license, or trade your data in any form.
  • We do not share data with partners, affiliates, or any other entity.

The only external system involved is Apple's HealthKit and the App Store distribution platform, both governed by Apple Inc.'s privacy practices, which you can review at apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. HEALTHKIT-SPECIFIC COMMITMENTS

Per Apple's HealthKit developer requirements, we explicitly affirm that BurnReward:

  • Will not use HealthKit data for advertising or advertising measurement purposes.
  • Will not disclose HealthKit data to third parties without user permission, except as required to provide or improve the app's health or fitness features.
  • Will not use HealthKit data to rebuild a user profile or to associate HealthKit data with other data for purposes beyond the app.
  • Will not sell HealthKit data to data brokers, insurers, financial institutions, or any third party.

These are hard requirements enforced by Apple, and we comply fully.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

BurnReward is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from any user and has no server-side data storage, there is no child data at risk. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using this app.

8. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

REVOKING HEALTHKIT ACCESS

You can revoke BurnReward's access to any HealthKit data type at any time through your Apple Watch: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → BurnReward. Revoking access will prevent the app from reading live calorie and heart rate data; you can still use the app but workout tracking will not function.

DELETING YOUR DATA

All BurnReward data stored on your device (quest progress, summaries, complication snapshots) is deleted automatically when you delete the app. You can also clear in-progress quest data at any time by tapping "NEW QUEST" on the earned screen. Workout records previously saved to Apple Health are managed independently through the Health app.

GDPR / EEA RESIDENTS

Because BurnReward processes no personal data on any server and transfers no data outside your device, the full GDPR data subject rights framework (access, rectification, erasure, portability) is satisfied by the on-device controls above. There is no server-side data to request, correct, or delete. For any questions, contact us at the address below.

CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS (CCPA)

BurnReward does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know what personal information we collect — and the honest answer is: none that leaves your device.

9. SECURITY

Because BurnReward stores data only on your device and never transmits it, the security of your health data is governed by the security of your Apple Watch itself (passcode, encryption at rest, Secure Enclave). We benefit from Apple's platform-level security protections and do not introduce additional data exposure vectors.

Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
