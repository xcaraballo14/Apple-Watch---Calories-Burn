PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: June 15, 2025  ·  Last Updated: July 22, 2026

1. OVERVIEW

BurnReward ("we", "our", or "the app") is an iPhone app with a bundled Apple Watch app, developed by Xavier Caraballo. This policy explains what information BurnReward accesses, how the app works on your own device, and — for the optional social features — what you share, with whom, and the controls you have over it.

The short version: You can play BurnReward entirely on your own device with no account — your quests, level, and records are computed from Apple Health on your iPhone and never leave it. If you choose to turn on the social features (the Guild), you create an account and share the game data you choose — including real workout metrics like calories and heart rate — with the party members you accept. You control what's visible, you can go private or delete your account at any time, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

This is an important change from earlier versions of this policy. BurnReward previously kept all health data on your device. The app now offers optional social features that share the data you choose. Everything below describes how that works.

2. HEALTH DATA WE ACCESS

BurnReward requests access to the following data types through Apple's HealthKit framework. This access, and how the data is used on your device, is the same whether or not you use the social features:

Data Type HealthKit Identifier Access Mode Why We Need It
Active Energy Burned HKQuantityTypeIdentifierActiveEnergyBurned READ Watch (live) · iPhone The core metric. The Watch compares your live calorie burn against your quest goal to show EXP progress and trigger milestones; the iPhone reads the calories on each saved workout to build your quest history, level, and records.
Heart Rate HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRate READ Watch (live) · iPhone Shown live during your workout and averaged at workout end for your summary; the iPhone reads that average for your quest receipt and XP.
Step Count HKQuantityTypeIdentifierStepCount READ Watch (live) · iPhone Shown live during a quest; the iPhone reads the step total on each saved workout for the quest detail and your "most steps" personal record.
Workout Sessions HKWorkoutType WRITE Watch · READ iPhone The Watch creates an HKWorkoutSession to run the sensors and writes the completed workout to Health when your quest is finished. The iPhone reads back only the workouts BurnReward saved, to reconstruct your quest history, level, badges, and records.

WHAT WE DO NOT ACCESS

BurnReward does not request and cannot access: body weight, blood oxygen, ECG data, sleep data, menstrual health data, reproductive health data, location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, or any other data type not listed above.

When you attach a photo to a post or pick a profile photo, you choose it through Apple's system photo picker, which hands BurnReward only the images you select — the app is never granted access to your whole photo library.

3. THE SOCIAL FEATURES — WHAT YOU SHARE

The social features (the "Guild") are entirely optional and off by default. You can use the whole game without them. They begin only when you sign in with Apple and are shown a consent screen describing exactly what is shared before you agree.

YOUR ACCOUNT

Signing in uses Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an anonymous account identifier and, if you allow it, a relay email address that forwards to you without revealing your real address. You then choose a public username. We never receive your Apple ID password or your real email unless you choose to share it.

WHAT YOUR PARTY SEES

"Your party" means the specific players whose friend requests you have accepted. Once you enable sharing, your party can see the game data you choose:

  • Your character sheet — level, title, class, XP, streaks, trophies, and your personal records. This includes real workout metrics: calories burned, average heart rate, and step counts. Controlled by the "Show my character to my party" setting.
  • Wins you post to the feed — a summary of a completed quest, an earned trophy, or a level-up, with an optional caption and up to three photos you attach. Posting is always an explicit action you take.
  • Reactions you leave on your party's posts.
  • Your weekly XP, if you opt into the weekly challenge leaderboard.

A limited public profile — your username, level, title, and earned badge names — is visible to other signed-in players so they can find and add you. This public profile never contains calories, heart rate, steps, or any raw health metric.

PHOTOS

Photos you attach to posts are processed on your device before upload: location and other metadata (EXIF) are stripped out, and the image is screened on-device for explicit content. Photos are stored privately and are visible only to your accepted party.

You are always in control. You choose what to share, you can turn any sharing off in Settings at any time (which removes the shared copy), and blocking a player hides everything between you in both directions.

4. HOW DATA IS STORED

ON YOUR DEVICES (ALWAYS)

Your quest history, level, badges, records, and challenges are recomputed from Apple Health on your iPhone each time you open the app — they are not stored on a server. The iPhone's on-device storage holds only things that can't be recomputed: your reward library, notification and sharing preferences, and small "already handled" markers (which badges/records were celebrated, when you last opened the alerts bell). Your Apple Watch stores in-progress and just-completed quest data on-device.

ON OUR BACKEND (ONLY WHAT YOU SHARE)

When you use the social features, the data you choose to share is stored on our backend, provided by Supabase (see Section 6). This includes your profile, your shared character snapshot, your feed posts and their photos, your reactions, your weekly scores, and your friend/block/report records. Access is enforced by database row-level security so that, for example, your character snapshot and photos are readable only by you and the party members you have accepted.

We derive everything from Apple Health on your device first, then upload only the summaries you share. The backend is a sharing layer — it is not the source of truth for your health data, and it never receives raw HealthKit samples.

5. HOW DATA IS USED

Data accessed or shared through BurnReward is used solely to provide the app's features:

  • Running your live workout, milestones, summary, and saving the completed workout to Apple Health.
  • Computing and showing your quest history, level, badges, records, and challenges on your device.
  • Displaying the game data you choose to share to the party members you accept, and showing you theirs.
  • Operating the feed, reactions, leaderboard, friend requests, blocking, and reporting.
  • Scheduling optional local reminders on your iPhone, if you turn them on.

We never use your health data for advertising, ad targeting, or measurement; we never sell it or hand it to sponsors, advertisers, data brokers, insurers, or financial institutions; and we never use it for analytics profiling or research. Sponsored challenges or events, should they ever exist, will never involve giving your health data to a sponsor.

6. THIRD PARTIES AND SUB-PROCESSORS

We do not sell or trade your data, and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Firebase, Amplitude, Crashlytics, or advertising frameworks). The only third parties involved in operating the app are:

  • SUB-PROCESSOR Supabase — provides the backend that stores what you share (authentication, database, and photo storage). Supabase processes this data on our behalf to operate the social features. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple — HealthKit, Sign in with Apple, and App Store distribution, governed by Apple Inc.'s privacy practices at apple.com/legal/privacy.

These providers are service providers acting on our instructions to run the app. They are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

7. HEALTHKIT-SPECIFIC COMMITMENTS

Per Apple's HealthKit developer requirements, we explicitly affirm that BurnReward:

  • Will not use HealthKit data for advertising or advertising-measurement purposes.
  • Will disclose HealthKit-derived data to others only with your consent and only to provide the app's fitness features — specifically, sharing the game data you choose with the party members you accept. We do not disclose it to any other third party for their own purposes.
  • Will not use HealthKit data to build a marketing profile or to associate it with other data for advertising.
  • Will not sell HealthKit data to data brokers, insurers, financial institutions, sponsors, advertisers, or any third party.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

BurnReward is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The social features involve sharing information with other users; if you are under 13, do not create an account, and please ask a parent or guardian before using this app. If we learn that we have collected account information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

9. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

CONTROLLING WHAT YOU SHARE

Every kind of sharing is optional and reversible in the app's Settings: turn off "Show my character to my party" to make your character sheet private, leave the weekly challenge to remove your score, delete any post you've made, and block any player to hide everything between you. Turning a share off removes the shared copy from our backend.

REVOKING HEALTHKIT ACCESS

You can revoke BurnReward's access to any HealthKit data type at any time: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → BurnReward. This controls both the iPhone and Apple Watch apps.

DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT AND DATA

You can delete your account from within the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, shared character, posts, photos, reactions, scores, and friend/block records from our backend. On-device data is deleted when you delete the app. Workout records previously saved to Apple Health are managed independently through the Health app. If you never created an account, there is no server-side data to delete.

GDPR / EEA RESIDENTS

Where GDPR applies, our lawful basis for processing the data you share is your consent (given at the social consent screen and by your in-app sharing choices) and the performance of the service you request. You may access, correct, export, or erase your shared data using the in-app controls above, or by contacting us. You may withdraw consent at any time by turning off sharing or deleting your account.

CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS (CCPA)

BurnReward does not sell your personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know what we collect (described above), and to request deletion using the in-app account-deletion control or by contacting us.

10. SECURITY

On-device data is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption at rest, Secure Enclave). Data you share is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored by our backend provider with access enforced by database row-level security, so that only you and the party members you accept can read your shared content. The key shipped in the app is a restricted publishable key that cannot bypass those access rules.
