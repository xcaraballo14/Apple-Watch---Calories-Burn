PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: June 15, 2025  ·  Last Updated: July 9, 2026

1. OVERVIEW

BurnReward ("we", "our", or "the app") is an iPhone app with a bundled Apple Watch app, developed by Xavier Caraballo. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains in plain terms what information BurnReward accesses, why we access it, where it stays, and — critically — what we never do with it.

The short version: BurnReward reads your health data on your Apple Watch and iPhone and processes it entirely on your own devices — never transmitted to any server, never shared with any third party, and never used for advertising. We do not collect, store, or see your personal information.

2. INFORMATION WE ACCESS

BurnReward requests access to the following data types through Apple's HealthKit framework:

Data Type HealthKit Identifier Access Mode Why We Need It
Active Energy Burned HKQuantityTypeIdentifierActiveEnergyBurned READ Watch (live) · iPhone The core metric. The Watch compares your live calorie burn against your quest goal to show EXP progress and trigger milestones; the iPhone reads the calories on each saved workout to build your quest history, level, and records.
Heart Rate HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRate READ Watch (live) · iPhone Shown live during your workout and averaged at workout end for your summary; the iPhone reads that average for your quest receipt and XP.
Step Count HKQuantityTypeIdentifierStepCount READ Watch (live) · iPhone Shown live during a quest; the iPhone reads the step total on each saved workout for the quest detail and your "most steps" personal record.
Workout Sessions HKWorkoutType WRITE Watch · READ iPhone The Watch creates an HKWorkoutSession to run the sensors and writes the completed workout to Health when your quest is finished. The iPhone reads back only the workouts BurnReward saved, to reconstruct your quest history, level, badges, and records.

WHAT WE DO NOT ACCESS

BurnReward does not request and cannot access: body weight, blood oxygen, ECG data, sleep data, menstrual health data, reproductive health data, location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal or sensitive data type not listed above.

If you add a profile photo, you pick it through Apple's system photo picker, which hands BurnReward only the single image you choose — the app is never granted access to your photo library. Your optional display name is text you type in. Both stay on your device (see Section 3).

3. HOW DATA IS STORED

BurnReward stores data only on your own devices — never on a server — using Apple's standard on-device storage.

ON YOUR APPLE WATCH

  • Workout progress (current calorie count, phase, elapsed time, selected rewards) — stored in UserDefaults on-device so your quest resumes if the app is closed mid-workout. Cleared when you start a new quest.
  • Workout summary (total duration, average heart rate, total calories) — stored on-device after a quest is completed and displayed on the earned screen. Cleared when you start a new quest.
  • Complication snapshot & reward library — the live quest snapshot and the reward list synced from your iPhone, kept in an App Group container shared with the watch-face complication. On-device only.

ON YOUR iPHONE

The iPhone stores no health data of its own — your quest history, level, badges, records, and weekly challenge are recomputed from Apple Health each time you open the app. Its on-device UserDefaults holds only:

  • Your profile — an optional display name you type and an optional profile photo you pick (saved as a small local image). Both stay on the device and are never uploaded.
  • Your reward library — the custom rewards you create in the Reward Forge and which built-ins you've hidden.
  • Notification preferences — which reminders you've turned on and the reminder time.
  • Small "already handled" markers — e.g. which badges and records were already celebrated, and when you last opened the alerts bell, so a notification or toast fires only once.

None of this data is ever uploaded to a server, transmitted over a network, or stored in the cloud by BurnReward. It lives entirely on your devices. The iPhone and Apple Watch exchange the reward library and live quest progress directly, device-to-device, with no server in between.

Completed workouts saved to Apple Health are stored and managed entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy and iCloud data practices. BurnReward has no access to workout records after they are written.

4. HOW DATA IS USED

Data accessed by BurnReward is used solely for the following in-app purposes:

  • Tracking live active calorie burn against your selected food reward goal (EXP progress bar).
  • Displaying your real-time heart rate and steps during a workout session.
  • Triggering haptic milestone notifications at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of your calorie goal.
  • Computing and displaying a workout summary (duration, average heart rate, calories) when your quest is complete.
  • Saving the completed workout to Apple Health so it counts toward your rings and workout history.
  • Updating the watch-face complication to show live quest progress.
  • Showing your quest history, level and title, badges, personal records, and weekly challenge on the iPhone — all recomputed from your saved workouts.
  • Scheduling optional local reminders (achievements, streak, and weekly-challenge nudges) on your iPhone, if you turn them on.

We do not use your health data for advertising, analytics, machine learning, profiling, research, or any purpose beyond the in-app features described above.

5. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

BurnReward does not share your data with anyone.

  • We do not have servers. There is no backend. No data leaves your device through BurnReward.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Firebase, Amplitude, Crashlytics, or equivalent).
  • We do not use any advertising SDKs or frameworks.
  • We do not sell, rent, license, or trade your data in any form.
  • We do not share data with partners, affiliates, or any other entity.
  • Your iPhone and Apple Watch sync directly through Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity and HealthKit workout mirroring — this is device-to-device, never through a BurnReward server.

The only external system involved is Apple's HealthKit and the App Store distribution platform, both governed by Apple Inc.'s privacy practices, which you can review at apple.com/legal/privacy.

6. HEALTHKIT-SPECIFIC COMMITMENTS

Per Apple's HealthKit developer requirements, we explicitly affirm that BurnReward:

  • Will not use HealthKit data for advertising or advertising measurement purposes.
  • Will not disclose HealthKit data to third parties without user permission, except as required to provide or improve the app's health or fitness features.
  • Will not use HealthKit data to rebuild a user profile or to associate HealthKit data with other data for purposes beyond the app.
  • Will not sell HealthKit data to data brokers, insurers, financial institutions, or any third party.

These are hard requirements enforced by Apple, and we comply fully.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

BurnReward is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from any user (an optional display name or profile photo you may add stays on your device) and has no server-side data storage, there is no child data at risk. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using this app.

8. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

REVOKING HEALTHKIT ACCESS

You can revoke BurnReward's access to any HealthKit data type at any time on your iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → BurnReward (this controls both the iPhone and Apple Watch apps). Revoking access will prevent the app from reading your calorie, heart rate, and step data; you can still open the app but quest tracking and history will not function.

DELETING YOUR DATA

All BurnReward data stored on your devices is deleted automatically when you delete the app — quest progress and summaries on the Apple Watch, and your profile (name and photo), reward library, and preferences on the iPhone. You can also clear in-progress quest data at any time by tapping "NEW QUEST" on the earned screen. Workout records previously saved to Apple Health are managed independently through the Health app.

GDPR / EEA RESIDENTS

Because BurnReward processes no personal data on any server and transfers no data outside your device, the full GDPR data subject rights framework (access, rectification, erasure, portability) is satisfied by the on-device controls above. There is no server-side data to request, correct, or delete. For any questions, contact us at the address below.

CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS (CCPA)

BurnReward does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know what personal information we collect — and the honest answer is: none that leaves your device.

9. SECURITY

Because BurnReward stores data only on your devices and never transmits it, the security of your health data is governed by the security of your Apple Watch and iPhone themselves (passcode, encryption at rest, Secure Enclave). We benefit from Apple's platform-level security protections and do not introduce additional data exposure vectors.
