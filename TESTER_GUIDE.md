# BurnReward — Trusted Circle Testing Guide

Thanks for being one of the first people to test BurnReward! You're testing the
*whole* thing now — the Apple Watch app plus the new iPhone companion that turns
it into a full RPG. First pass takes about 15–20 minutes, then just use it
normally for a few days and see what breaks.

## What you need
- An iPhone (iOS 17.6+) with an Apple Watch paired (Series 4 or later, watchOS 10.6+)
- The TestFlight app (from the App Store)
- The invite link Xavier sent you

---

## 1. Install
- [ ] Open the TestFlight invite link → **Install** BurnReward on your iPhone
- [ ] Open the **Watch app** on your iPhone → My Watch → scroll down → install
      BurnReward there too (it's a separate install now that there's a phone app)
- [ ] Open BurnReward on your **Watch** first

## 2. Run your first quest (Watch)
- [ ] Tap **Allow** on the Health access prompt (needed to track your workout —
      nothing leaves your device)
- [ ] Pick a reward — pick something you actually want to eat later, it's more
      fun that way. You can pick a second one for a combo quest.
- [ ] Choose a workout type (Walk / Run / Bike / Lift / Other) and start
- [ ] Go move for real — walk the block, whatever gets your heart rate up
- [ ] Watch the bar fill as you go — you should feel a little buzz at 25%, 50%,
      and 75%
- [ ] Hit 100% → victory screen with your time, avg heart rate, and calories

**Also try:** close the app mid-quest (side button, or swipe it away) and
reopen it — your quest should still be right where you left it.

## 3. Check the iPhone app
Open BurnReward on your phone after finishing (or *during* — a live quest
should show up on Home while you're still mid-workout).

- [ ] **HOME** — your finished quest shows as "Last Quest," your level bar moved
- [ ] **LOG** — the quest is in your history with the right calories and time
- [ ] **CHARACTER** — check your class (Strider/Wayfarer/Outrider/Juggernaut,
      depending on workout type), then scroll to the trophy case — locked
      badges should look like grayed-out versions of the real medallion, not a
      mystery box
- [ ] Tap into the quest from LOG — you should see an itemized XP receipt
      (base calories, intensity bonus, and a precision bonus if you landed
      close to your goal)

## 4. A few extra things worth trying
- [ ] **FORGE** — create a custom reward (any food + calorie count), then check
      your Watch — it should show up in the picker there automatically
- [ ] **Notifications** — turn them on in Settings (CHARACTER → gear icon),
      then earn a badge or level up with your phone locked — you should get a
      notification
- [ ] **Dark mode** — switch your phone to dark mode and poke around, make
      sure nothing looks broken
- [ ] **The bell** (top right of Home) — shows your recent achievements and
      how close you are to your next one

---

## How to send feedback
Easiest: screenshot + TestFlight's built-in feedback (shake your phone, or the
feedback tab in TestFlight) — it attaches the screenshot and some diagnostics
automatically.

Or email **burnrewardapp@gmail.com** with what you expected vs. what actually
happened, and what iPhone/Watch you're on.

Nothing is too small — weird wording, a button that's hard to tap, numbers
that don't look right, anything. Thanks for helping test this!
